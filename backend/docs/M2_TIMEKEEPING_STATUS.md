## M2.5.1 leave credit milestone — pending integrated acceptance
- V40 introduces tenant-RLS policy history and once-only employee/month/type credit postings. Database constraints enforce numeric 0–31 days, first-of-month periods and uniqueness.
- API: `POST /api/v1/timekeeping/leave-credit-policies` with {leaveTypeId,monthlyCreditDays,effectiveFrom}; `GET /leave-credit-policies?leaveTypeId=...`; `POST /employees/{employeeId}/leave-credits/accrue?leaveTypeId=...&month=YYYY-MM`; `GET /employees/{employeeId}/leave-credits?leaveTypeId=...`; `GET /employees/{employeeId}/leave-credit-balances` under the `/api/v1/timekeeping` prefix.
- Employee row locking and uniqueness reject double postings, and posting a future month or pre-hire month is blocked. Posted entries are immutable through the public API and audited.
- The balance is **informational accrued credits**, not available-to-take days. There is no balance subtraction for approved leave, no proration, eligibility, carryover, expiration, policy-version retroactivity, year-end close or backdated employment replay.
- This is not a representation of Philippine statutory leave entitlement and must not affect employee wages, legal leave or payroll until reviewed by HR/legal and tested.
- PostgreSQL live HTTP regression covers policy create, duplicate denial, future rejection, single posting, audit and read-only credit aggregation. New CI verification is required before marking this increment accepted.

## M2.5.2 manual leave reconciliation and carryover preview (in certification)
- V41 stores tenant-isolated append-only DEBIT and compensating REVERSAL events tied to approved leave requests. An approved request can produce at most one debit, and a debit at most one reversal, enforced by database unique indexes.
- The POST `/api/v1/timekeeping/employees/{employeeId}/leave-balances/requests/{requestId}/consume` body is `{ "days":1.000, "note":"HR reviewed chargeable day" }`. Its days are explicit HR input, must not exceed request's calendar span or available informational credits. Employee row locking serializes competing credit postings/debits/reversals; a request's approval must already be recorded.
- The POST `/api/v1/timekeeping/employees/{employeeId}/leave-balances/events/{eventId}/reverse` body is `{ "note":"Approved adjustment" }`. It never deletes the original debit. Read-only `GET /.../leave-balances` shows accrued, consumed and remaining **informational** days; `GET /.../leave-balances/events?leaveTypeId=...` exposes the journal.
- V42 adds capped carryover **planning only**: tenant-specific policy with next-year cap, and a read-only preview using the current unsettled ledger. `POST /api/v1/timekeeping/leave-carryover-preview-policies` configures `{leaveTypeId,destinationYear,maximumDays}`; `GET /api/v1/timekeeping/employees/{employeeId}/leave-carryover-preview?leaveTypeId=...&destinationYear=...` shows a `posted:false` preview. It does not create, transfer or expire credits.
- This phase explicitly does NOT calculate chargeable working days, half-days, rest-day/holiday exclusion, Philippine statutory entitlements, policy year-end close, protected negative-balance exceptions, payable leave or payroll integration. Human HR/legal review and production data acceptance remain blocked.
- Live PostgreSQL HTTP tests cover approved debit, replay denial, reversal, double reversal denial, audit events, remaining balance and carryover cap/no-mutation; final integrated CI must pass before acceptance.

## M2.6 company holidays and rest days — provisional classification
- Flyway V43 adds company-scoped calendar dates (HOLIDAY, SPECIAL_NON_WORKING, COMPANY_CLOSURE) and employee-specific REST_DAY/WORK_DAY overrides. Each table has forced tenant RLS, company/employee foreign keys, a one-per-date uniqueness rule, actor attribution and audit events.
- The REST API under `/api/v1/timekeeping` adds POST and GET `/calendar-days`, POST and GET `/employees/{employeeId}/day-overrides`, and read-only GET `/employees/{employeeId}/calendar-preview`. Date range queries are capped at 31 days.
- Preview applies effective-dated shift weekday masks, then employee overrides. An unscheduled employee is `UNSCHEDULED`, **not** automatically on a rest day. Company holidays and employee rest-day overrides are displayed separately so both can coexist. Existing attendance and leave records are not altered.
- The React admin screen at `/calendar` is reachable from `/timekeeping`, and guarded through existing timekeeping authentication/MFA. Browser smoke tests validate navigation and unauthenticated API access refusal.
- Live PostgreSQL HTTP tests verify weekday/weekend classification, employer holiday overlay, employee rest-day exception, duplicate denial, audit events and date range bounds. Latest M2.6 CI must pass before this release increment is marked verified.
- **Not implemented or certified:** government-maintained holiday catalog; jurisdiction/location-specific holiday classifications; employee/branch regional overrides; actual statutory rest-day, OT or holiday pay rules; automatic chargeable leave-day computation; payroll handoff. Policy preview remains `INFORMATIONAL_NO_LEGAL_HOLIDAY_OR_PAYROLL_CALCULATION`.

## M2.7 attendance/leave reconciliation and internal payroll handoff preparation
- V44 adds a tenant-RLS-controlled, employee- and date-range-keyed, immutable-source reconciliation package. It stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of the source snapshot and an independent review audit. The package status is DRAFT or INTERNALLY_REVIEWED, never payroll approved.
- `GET /api/v1/timekeeping/employees/{employeeId}/payroll-reconciliation/preview?from=...&to=...&timezone=...`: live, timezone-aware raw attendance source totals and blockers for a maximum 31-day date range.
- `POST /.../payroll-reconciliation/packages` prepares an internal source snapshot with explicit blocker codes. Duplicate employee/period packages are rejected.
- `GET /.../payroll-reconciliation/packages` lists internal snapshots without revealing the raw source string.
- `POST /.../payroll-reconciliation/packages/{packageId}/review` requires a distinct MFA-verified actor, zero blocker codes and an unchanged source fingerprint. A stale or blocked package cannot be reviewed. This does **not** allow a payroll run or external export.
- Blockers include open attendance sessions, pending attendance corrections, pending leave decisions, approved leave without active reconciliation debit, and provisional company calendar classifications. Raw closed minutes are clipped to the requested zone/date window, not payable or deduplicated hours; the M2.3 variance preview remains a separate input.
- The React `/reconciliation` screen provides source-preview, internal package and independent-review controls. M2.7 Playwright and PostgreSQL HTTP integration tests verify authentication, blocker gates, audit and no-payroll semantics.
- **Not implemented:** full multi-employee payroll batch orchestration, full anomaly resolution, payable hour calculations, overtime authorization, statutory holiday premiums, tax/social contribution calculation, payroll-provider export, payroll release or payroll accounting journal. Production remains NO-GO.

## M2.8 reports, controlled audit export and retention planning
- Flyway V45 introduces `time_reporting_export_receipt`, with tenant-RLS, actor, report category, bounded date range, record count, and SHA-256 digest of the generated CSV; plus `time_retention_plan` with forced tenant isolation, company ownership, minimum 365-day retention and mandatory `legal_hold=true`.
- `GET /api/v1/timekeeping/reports/attendance.csv?companyId=...&from=...&to=...&timezone=...` returns at most 500 clipped raw attendance session rows for up to 31 days. Columns include only controlled UUID, timestamp, raw minutes, open indicator and `RAW_NOT_PAYROLL_APPROVED`. It is not a payable-hours calculation.
- `GET /api/v1/timekeeping/reports/audit.csv?from=...&to=...` provides at most 500 server-originated timekeeping audit events, excluding employee names, free-text reasons, and email addresses. String columns are CSV-escaped and spreadsheet-formula guarded. Audit windows are UTC.
- Both CSV endpoints require tenant admin + MFA and return `Cache-Control: no-store`, `Content-Disposition: attachment`. Each successful export produces a separate export receipt and audit event. `GET /api/v1/timekeeping/reports/export-receipts` lists the latest 100 receipts for the tenant.
- `POST /api/v1/timekeeping/retention/plans` records one immutable, company-scoped, legal-hold-on advisory policy; a duplicate or shortened replacement is rejected. `GET /retention/plans?companyId=...` lists policies. `GET /retention/preview?companyId=...` counts old closed attendance sessions only, always returns `deletionEnabled=false`, and intentionally leaves audit deletion candidates at zero because audit company attribution is ambiguous.
- There is **no delete/purge/expiration endpoint**, automatic retention job, external data recipient or legal-hold bypass. Actual retention periods require legal/privacy/HR approval and incident response policy; records and payroll are unchanged.
- Frontend `/timekeeping-reports` provides authorized downloads, receipt lookup and non-destructive retention planning. Browser tests check protected UI and unauthenticated export refusal. HTTP integration tests cover CSV headers, bounded results, checksum receipts, invalid windows/zones, mandatory legal hold and refusal to shorten an existing plan.
- Full multi-role and cross-tenant abuse testing, high-volume export throughput, encrypted archive/presigned delivery, and formal legal retention acceptance remain outstanding M2.10 gates.

## M2.9 employee self-service foundation — read-only, devices explicitly NO-GO
- V46 creates `time_employee_identity_link` as a forced-tenant-RLS, unique identity-to-employee mapping, provisioned only by a tenant administrator with MFA. The link cannot be asserted by email matching, self-submitted employee UUID or a client-provided tenant. Only enabled identities with the EMPLOYEE role and active/on-leave personnel records may be bound; an administrator cannot bind their own identity.
- V47 adds recorded link revocation (`revoked_at` and `revoked_by`), preserving audit evidence. A revoked link is excluded from employee `GET /api/v1/employee-self-service/me` immediately, even if the bearer token is still valid. Rebinding remains forbidden by lifetime uniqueness; remediation requires a separately approved future workflow.
- `POST /api/v1/employee-self-service/admin/identity-links` provisions a one-to-one link. Admin-only `GET /admin/identity-links?employeeId=...` finds an active link, and `POST /admin/identity-links/{linkId}/revoke` disables it. These routes are guarded by both TENANT_ADMIN and MFA_VERIFIED.
- Employee-only `GET /api/v1/employee-self-service/me` resolves the current identity from the authenticated principal, not from URL/body inputs. It returns a minimum-data employee profile, schedule assignments and leave statuses, limited to 100 rows per category. Only EMPLOYEE + MFA_VERIFIED may invoke it, and terminated/retired/suspended personnel are denied.
- `/my-timekeeping` is the employee-facing React view. `/employee-access` is the restricted HR administrative linking/revocation screen. Browser tests verify no employee mutation controls and anonymous refusal; live PostgreSQL HTTP tests use disposable synthetic MFA-assured identities to verify unlinked denial, verified read, employee admin-route denial, no cross-surface access, revocation, replay rejection, and audit trails.
- `time_ess_device_policy` intentionally has fail-closed database constraints: employee clock-in, GPS/location collection and biometrics are all **false**. No device enrollment, attendance device tokens, geofencing, capture/consent or revocation workflow is enabled. Admin clock in/out from M2.2 is a separate MFA-admin-only operation.
